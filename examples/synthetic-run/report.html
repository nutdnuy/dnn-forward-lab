04 / Method, provenance and limits

Four independent feed-forward networks use 5 lagged prices, two ReLU hidden layers, Adam and L1 loss. Scaling uses only fitting data. The trailing 30 historical bars select epochs by recursive validation MAE; those bars do not update weights.

Long/cash, fractional shares, no leverage. A signal at close executes at the next open. Commission: 5.0 bps per side. Slippage: 5.0 bps per side. Terminal liquidation is scheduled at each fold's last close. Taxes, financing, volume limits and cash interest are excluded.
